Welcome to Aero Sentry

Aero Sentry is an interactive, real-time aviation tracking platform designed specifically to monitor military and civilian airspace across the United Kingdom. Operating as a live digital radar dashboard, it uses Automatic Dependent Surveillance-Broadcast (ADS-B) telemetry to give aviation enthusiasts, plane spotters and local residents total situational awareness of the skies above them.

How it works

The platform simplifies complex transponder telemetry into a live, interactive map dashboard in three main steps:

  1. Signal capture. Aero Sentry continuously scans the skies, capturing live data transmitted directly from civilian and military aircraft.
  2. Localised anchor. Enter any UK postcode to instantly centre the radar scope on your exact geographic area and see exactly what is flying overhead.
  3. Two-tier proximity alerts. You don't need to stare at the screen to catch the action. A built-in smart audio engine broadcasts live military movement based on how close it is.

Proximity alerts

35 NM

The perimeter — screen ping

When an active military transponder is detected within a 35 nautical mile radius, the platform issues an audible ping to announce its entry into your tracking zone.

5 NM

Close range — voice callouts

When a military aircraft gets within the close-range 5 NM boundary, the system upgrades to automated voice callouts, verbally broadcasting its current heading, altitude, speed and approach direction.

  • Aviation emergency alerts. Real-time audio callouts for all major squawk codes.
  • Military intercepts. Integrated audio tracking notifications for QRA fighter deployments.

Dashboard features & data points

The live tracking board isolates active transponders to decode and display critical flight kinematics in real time:

Identity & route
Aircraft type, registration, departure point and destination.
Positioning metrics
Exact range (in NM), altitude (in feet), bearing and heading degrees.
Motion metrics
Speed (in knots), vertical speed rate (V/S), total flight time and Estimated Time of Arrival (ETA).
