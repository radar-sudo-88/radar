Welcome to Aero Sentry

Aero Sentry is an interactive, real-time aviation tracking platform designed specifically to monitor military and civilian airspace across the United Kingdom. Operating as a live digital radar dashboard, it uses Automatic Dependent Surveillance-Broadcast (ADS-B) telemetry to give aviation enthusiasts, plane spotters and local residents total situational awareness of the skies above them.

How it works

The platform simplifies complex transponder telemetry into a live, interactive map dashboard in three main steps:

  1. Signal capture. Aero Sentry continuously scans the skies, capturing live data transmitted directly from civilian and military aircraft.
  2. Localised anchor. Enter any UK postcode to instantly centre the radar scope on your exact geographic area and see exactly what is flying overhead.
  3. Two-tier proximity alerts. You don't need to stare at the screen to catch the action. A built-in smart audio engine broadcasts live military movement based on how close it is.

Proximity alerts

35 NM

The perimeter — screen ping

When an active military transponder is detected within a 35 nautical mile radius, the platform issues an audible ping to announce its entry into your tracking zone.

5 NM

Close range — voice callouts

When a military aircraft gets within the close-range 5 NM boundary, the system upgrades to automated voice callouts, verbally broadcasting its current heading, altitude, speed and approach direction.

  • Aviation emergency alerts. Real-time audio callouts for all major squawk codes.
  • Military intercepts. Integrated audio tracking notifications for QRA fighter deployments.

Dashboard features & data points

The live tracking board isolates active transponders to decode and display critical flight kinematics in real time:

Identity & route
Aircraft type, registration, departure point and destination.
Positioning metrics
Exact range (in NM), altitude (in feet), bearing and heading degrees.
Motion metrics
Speed (in knots), vertical speed rate (V/S), total flight time and Estimated Time of Arrival (ETA).

Weather radar overlay

The scope shows more than aircraft. A live rain radar layer sits underneath the blips, so you can watch weather moving through your area alongside the traffic.

  • Source. Precipitation radar from RainViewer's public weather-maps data.
  • Always current. The newest available radar frame is loaded automatically and swapped in every 10 minutes, with no page reload. It shows recently observed rain and snow — it is not a forecast.
  • Kept out of the way. It is drawn as a soft, translucent backdrop under the aircraft, so blips, labels and the sweep stay readable. Where the shading is stronger, the precipitation is heavier.
  • Approximate detail. The free radar tiles are coarse, so at the 35 NM scope the shading looks blocky. It shows where rain is falling, not exactly where its edges are.
  • Not for weather warnings. For official warnings and forecasts, use the Met Office.

Install the app

Aero Sentry can be installed like an app, opening full-screen from your home screen or desktop.

iPhone / iPad: tap Share, then “Add to Home Screen”. Android / Chrome: open the browser menu and choose “Install app” or “Add to Home screen”.

Disclaimer

Aero Sentry is for information and enjoyment only. It must not be used for navigation, flight planning, air traffic purposes, or any safety-critical decision.

ADS-B data comes from volunteer-run networks and can be delayed, incomplete or wrong. Aircraft may be missing, mislabelled or shown in the wrong place, and some aircraft — military ones in particular — don't broadcast, or aren't shown. Alerts are best-effort and are not guaranteed. The weather radar is approximate and can be around 10 minutes old.

This is an independent project. It is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, any airline, air force, air navigation service provider or data source named above. You use it at your own risk.

Privacy: your postcode is stored only in a cookie on your own device, so the radar remembers your area. It is sent to postcodes.io to be turned into map coordinates.
